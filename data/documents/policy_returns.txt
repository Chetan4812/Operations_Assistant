Policy Name: Return Policy
Last Updated: 2026-01-15

Returns are permitted within 30 days of the delivery date.
- Items must be in their original packaging and unused condition.
- A 15% restocking fee is applied to opened hardware products (including Widget A).
- Software and digital products are non-refundable.
- Return shipping costs are paid by the customer unless the item was delivered damaged or defective.
- Refund processing takes 5 to 7 business days from receipt of the item at our warehouse.
