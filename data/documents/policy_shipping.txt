Policy Name: Shipping Policy
Last Updated: 2026-02-10

We offer three tiers of shipping services:
1. Standard Shipping: 3-5 business days. Free for orders over $50.00, otherwise flat rate of $5.99.
2. Express Shipping: 1-2 business days. Flat rate of $15.00.
3. Priority Overnight: Next business day delivery. Flat rate of $35.00.

Orders are processed within 24 hours of placement (excluding weekends and holidays). 
Once shipped, customers receive a tracking ID via email. 
International shipping is currently unavailable.
